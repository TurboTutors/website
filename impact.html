Definitions

How We Define Our Metrics

We define each metric clearly so the numbers are honest and comparable over time.

Students Supported

Unique individual students who have participated in at least one completed TurboTutors session. If a student books and attends multiple sessions, they are counted once.

Tutoring Hours

Total tutor time delivered in completed sessions. One 60-minute appointment counts as one hour. Does not include scheduling time or email communication.

Sessions Completed

Total booked appointments that took place as scheduled. No-shows, student cancellations, and tutor cancellations are excluded from this count.

Data Source

Session records are maintained through TurboTutors' scheduling system. All impact data is reviewed and updated periodically by organization leadership.

Data Note: Impact figures are self-reported based on TurboTutors' internal session records. Data is updated as new sessions are completed and reviewed by leadership. Last updated August 2026.
