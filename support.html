Our Commitment

Tutoring stays free for students.

Contributions help us sustain and expand the resources needed to support our educational mission. From curriculum development to organizational infrastructure, your support makes continued free tutoring possible.

No donation is ever required to receive tutoring.

Students access TurboTutors sessions at zero cost regardless of whether the organization receives donations.

Contributions supporting TurboTutors are administered through our fiscal sponsor, the Institute for Education, Research, and Scholarships (IFERS), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. IFERS provides administrative oversight for charitable contributions made to advance TurboTutors' educational mission.
